## 2026-11-08 Grand Park update

- dist/grandpark-data.json: manually researched public reference snapshot, field schema, source URLs, conflicts and eight synthetic units.
- dist/grandpark.js: bilingual research directory, orthogonal inventory filters and simulated onboarding checks.
- dist/grandpark.css: responsive styles for the new sections.
- index.html and en.html retain the original financial illustration and incident UI; all actions are simulations.

The directory includes 10 subdivisions/developments, not a certified complete master plan. Published area/tower/unit figures are not reconciled legal inventory. Never derive individual unit IDs, ownership, current stock or rent from project totals. Origami tower references are published layouts, not evidence of specific units or floor counts. Missing measurements remain null. Area bases must be distinguished: land, gross floor, net internal.

Reference scale: 271 ha / ~23% building density / 36 ha park / 71 published towers. All source check dates are 2026-11-08. Do not treat this as live market data. Source totals and district labels can be historical. Exact legal unit data and approved planning documents have not been obtained.

Conflict handling: Rainbow summary total differs from cluster sum; Beverly lists 6 and 10 towers within the same page; Origami counts/ranges differ; Opus launch area >2.3 ha differs from Market 23 ha. Opus is excluded pending completion verification, not assumed handed over based on a forecast.

Asset scope: apartment and shophouse; podium and low-rise shops have separate subtype fields. Developer/private owner, primary-sale history, listing availability, lease, actual receipt, physical state and tokenization state are independent.

Available is a candidate for onboarding, not a legal authorization to issue tokens. Owner consent, sale authority, title/encumbrances, actual completion, legal structure and valuation must be checked. Simulated checks never assert legal ownership. Existing-owner sale proceeds are not automatically new developer capital. No platform utility/governance token; no actual issuance, wallet connection, investment collection, partnership or bank integration.

Validation in this revision: JSON/source consistency; 10 subdivisions and 21 Origami tower references; 8 explicitly fictional units; browser checks of VI/EN, combined shophouse+available+leased filters, developer filter, reset, simulated approval and unavailable blocking. No real asset or onchain tests performed.

## Workflow explainer v0.3

`dist/workflows.js` and `.css` provide four VI/EN walkthroughs: ownership/issuance, rent/fees, incidents, and valuation/transfers. Each step identifies the responsible party, offchain/attestation/onchain layer, and proposed record. Simulated completion adds a local-only log; route changes and reset clear it. Early issuance/distribution attempts display blocked conditions. Acquisition routes include partial retention, full sale, and Propertichain acquisition first. No bank, sponsor or legal guarantee is implied. Service fees are included in disclosed costs, with no extra fee schedule invented.

Roadmap v1.1: iterative pilot acceptance precedes internal developer-group rollout, which precedes external B2B sales. On-site property-specific purchase flow remains planned; no live checkout or wallet/payment integration.
